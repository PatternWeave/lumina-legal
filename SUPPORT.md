# LightWeave Support

LightWeave is a Philips Hue lighting companion for **iPhone and iPad**.

## Help

- **No Bridge found** — stay on the home Wi‑Fi. Grant Local Network when iOS asks. Press the button on the Hue Bridge when the app says to.
- **Away from home** — sign in with your Hue account. The Hue developer app callback is `lightweave://oauth`.
- **Voice** — named rooms and lights parse on this iPhone. Optional Phi-3 Mini is a one-time on-device download for vaguer phrasing. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Purchases** — Restore purchases is in Settings. Manage Voice Unlimited in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
