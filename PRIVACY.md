# Lumina Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Lumina (“the app”) is a lighting companion for Philips Hue. It is designed so you can speak to Grok and change lights in your home.

## What Lumina collects

Lumina does not create an account. It does not run ads or analytics SDKs.

On this device the app stores:

- Apple purchase account token and usage totals (UserDefaults)
- Hue Bridge address and application key (Keychain / UserDefaults)
- Hue OAuth access and refresh tokens if you enable anywhere access (Keychain)

You do not need an xAI developer account. Lumina Voice is an in-app purchase. Grok API calls are made by Lumina Cloud using the publisher’s key.

## Audio

Microphone audio and typed lighting commands are sent to xAI (Grok) **only after you agree** on the in-app consent screen. You can withdraw that consent in Settings. Tapping rooms and scenes does not call Grok. Streaming stops when you end a Voice session. Purchase receipts are processed by Apple. xAI’s processing is described in the [xAI privacy policy](https://x.ai/legal/privacy-policy).

## Lighting control

Commands are sent to your Philips Hue Bridge on the local network, or to the Hue Remote API (`api.meethue.com`) when you sign in with Hue. Signify’s processing is described in the [Philips Hue privacy policy](https://www.philips-hue.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Permissions

- **Microphone** — talk to Grok.
- **Local network** — find the Hue Bridge on Wi‑Fi.

You can deny either permission. Without the microphone, you can still type commands. Without local network, you can still use Hue Remote sign-in.

## Third parties

- xAI (Grok Voice and Grok text APIs)
- Signify / Philips Hue (Bridge and Remote API)

Lumina does not sell personal data.

## Safety

You can report a Grok reply, pause Grok, or turn off sharing in Settings. Email support from the app. Lumina is a lighting controller, not a social network; there are no other users to block.

## Subscriptions

Voice Unlimited (`app.lumina.hue.voice.monthly`) is an auto-renewable subscription billed through Apple. It renews each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Pro (`app.lumina.hue.pro`) is a one-time purchase. Restore purchases is in Lumina Settings.

## Contact

Email support@lumina.app. You can also use Settings → Safety → Email support in the app.
