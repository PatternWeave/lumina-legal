Lumina Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Lumina is a lighting companion for Philips Hue. You can speak to Grok and change lights in your home.

What Lumina collects

Lumina does not create an account. It does not run ads or analytics SDKs.

On this device the app stores:

  • Apple purchase account token and usage totals
  • Hue Bridge address and application key
  • Hue OAuth access and refresh tokens if you enable anywhere access

You do not need an xAI developer account. Lumina Voice is an in-app purchase. Grok API calls are made by Lumina Cloud using the publisher’s key.

Audio and typed commands

Microphone audio and typed lighting commands are sent to xAI (Grok) only after you agree on the in-app “Before you talk to Grok” screen. You can withdraw that consent in Settings. Tapping rooms and scenes does not call Grok. Streaming stops when you end a Voice session. Purchase receipts are processed by Apple. See the xAI privacy policy.

Lighting control

Lumina uses the official Philips Hue CLIP API on your local network (Bridge button press) and the official Hue Remote API (OAuth) when you sign in. See the Philips Hue privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Microphone — talk to Grok.
  • Local network — find the Hue Bridge on Wi‑Fi.

You can deny either permission. Without the microphone, you can still type commands. Without local network, you can still use Hue Remote sign-in.

Third parties

  • xAI (Grok Voice and Grok text APIs)
  • Signify / Philips Hue (Bridge and Remote API)
  • Apple (App Store purchases and subscriptions)

Lumina does not sell personal data.

Safety

You can report a Grok reply, pause Grok for 24 hours, or turn off sharing in Settings. Lumina is a lighting controller, not a social network. There are no other users to block.

Subscriptions

Voice Unlimited (app.lumina.hue.voice.monthly) is an auto-renewable subscription billed through Apple at $4.99 per month. It renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Pro (app.lumina.hue.pro) is a $3.99 one-time purchase. Restore purchases is in Lumina Settings.

Contact / support

Email support@lumina.app. You can also use Settings → Safety → Email support in the app.
